Information

The Big Squeeze began in 2014 with the mission to honor and support our local veterans. Businesses and organizations across the county erect and decorate lemonade stands to solicit monetary donations. All funds raised from the lemonade stands are donated to our VFW and American Legion chapters. Over the years the festival grew with the addition of events and activities. In 2026 The Big Squeeze became a two-day festival.

Q: Where can I park for the festival?

Parking is available at designated public lots in both Parsons and Decaturville. Follow event signage upon arrival.

Q: What happens if it rains?

The festival runs rain or shine! In the case of severe weather, updates will be posted to the News feed.

Q: Are pets allowed?

Leashed pets are welcome in outdoor common areas, but please check specific vendor locations for individual restrictions.
